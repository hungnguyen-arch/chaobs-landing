SKTT - The Tech Hub of Hello Doctor Ecosystem

1. ABOUT US

Founded in early 2024, SKTT is an early-stage, AI-first MedTech startup operating within the Hello Doctor healthcare network. We specialize in building next-generation EMR platforms and automated clinical triage solutions for mental health and neurology.

2. OUR CORE AI TECHNOLOGIES

We build a highly scalable, secure data and AI infrastructure on the cloud:

🩺

AI Clinical Triage

Integrating LLMs into messaging channels to take patient history, assess tests (PHQ-9), and alert dangerous risks.

🎙️

Vocal Biomarkers

Mining audio data from CS calls using Machine Learning to analyze patient stress levels without costly hardware.

🧠

RAG Clinical Decision Support

A Vector database built from thousands of regimens, helping doctors lookup drug interactions in seconds.
